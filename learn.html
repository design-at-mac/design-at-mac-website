More than a record of achievement.

Understand reflective practice, scholarship, student learning, evidence, disciplinary context, and the red thread.

Core characteristics

What makes a portfolio pedagogic?

A pedagogic portfolio is not an extended CV or collection of praise. It is a selective, evidence-informed account of how the educator understands learning, explains decisions, evaluates consequences, and develops over time.

Reflective practice

Analyze concrete experiences rather than merely report activity.

Scholarly engagement

Use relevant literature to deepen interpretation and test assumptions.

Learning focus

Examine what students are trying to understand or do.

Disciplinary context

Explain field-specific learning hurdles and professional reasoning.

Evidence and authenticity

Connect narrative, authentic artifacts, and other perspectives.

Development over time

Show how professional judgment evolves through iteration.

The red thread

Make the reasoning easy to follow

The red thread connects teaching values, a student-learning problem, interpretation, action, evidence, consequences, learning, and the next step. Readers should not have to guess how the parts relate.

A practical test

Because I believe ___, when I noticed ___, I interpreted the issue using ___, took the action of ___, examined ___ as evidence, learned ___, and will next ___.
