Learn why the initiative was created, how the pilot developed, and what faculty and reviewers learned.

Purpose

Why the initiative exists

The initiative addresses a gap between teaching portfolios assembled mainly for summative decisions and portfolio work used for reflective professional learning. The pilot has supported focused writing, expert feedback, peer review, online resources, and the development of a continuing mentoring community.

Reflective practice

Make changes in teaching understanding visible, including mixed outcomes and failure.

Scholarliness

Use educational and discipline-based scholarship to interpret practice.

Student learning

Focus on learning problems, conditions, evidence, and consequences.

Pedagogical reasoning

Explain the rationale connecting values, evidence, action, and future development.
